# The lens ring: the piece fills the screen, smoked glass hugs its four edges

atelier's live view is «the lens ring», picked by dima on 2026-09-30 after three design rounds (brief v1, full, blind). The piece is the biggest thing on screen. Four smoked-glass edges hug it, one per setting group: light on top, lens on the right, atmosphere at the bottom, toggles on the left. One edge opens at a time (`1`–`4`, `esc` closes it), and the corners hold the actions: the piece and its seed top-left, time and commands top-right, the takes stack bottom-left, the view tools and the bake button bottom-right. `g` opens the takes stack into a film strip along the bottom. A flat piece sits on the plain ground at a whole zoom, and a favicon opens a pixel view at the sizes it lands at (16, 32, 64 px), with a pixel grid from 8×. Below 1100 px the ring unrolls into four tabs under the art.

The design source is the studio job `jobs/atelier/` (`contract.md`, `decision.md`, the canvas comp); the build is [BYT-113](https://linear.app/x-com/issue/BYT-113).

## Considered options

- **glass-familiar, the lightroom bench** — solid, but the classic layout. Its film strip lives on as the ring's opened takes stack.
- **swiss-familiar, the ruled bench** — «i like swiss too. but glass better»: flat panels beside the art took width from it.
- **swiss-experimental, the spec sheet** — same material call; the spec sheet under the art pushed the piece up and made it smaller.
- **the adhd and vague spreads** — no take picked; three ideas borrowed: type to find any setting (`/`), the piece shown where it lands (the pixel view's true sizes), and take state by shape rather than colour (a stashed take has a dashed edge).

## Consequences

- one open edge is the rule, and the film strip counts as the bottom edge: opening one folds the other.
- the keys stay the ones dima already uses (`n` day and night, `t` theme, `w` the readme frame) over the comp's labels, except the comp's `p` for the pieces list and `m` for motion (dima's review, 2026-09-30); the ring adds `1`–`4`, `esc`, `g`, `/`, `e` (new seed), `y` (copy json) and `r` (reset, with a 5 s undo).
- the piece is fitted whole, never cropped: an 8:3 scene on a 16:10 screen leaves the ground above and below it (dima, 2026-09-30: «an art tool should never hide the art»). An open edge sits in that ground when it has room, and reaches into the art only as far as its own height forces.
- the glass is one smoke in both themes; the theme picks the ground behind a day piece, and a night piece always sits on the dark ground.
- the top and bottom edges sit on the piece's rim, not the window's edge, so the glass always overlaps the art.
- the direction came from the studio's three design rounds (`decision.md`), not from impeccable's concept roll, so it carries no seed key.
- revisit when a piece needs two groups open at once in daily use, when a group passes ~10 settings (a side edge holds about 10 rows before it scrolls), or when glass text is hard to read over a bright flat piece (the smoke may need raising).
