# cv

dima's visit card: a short cv and a cover letter, framed as a small browser window.

## Language

**Browser frame**:
The window drawing every page sits inside: three dots, the page tabs and the theme toggle.
_Avoid_: shell, chrome, layout

**Brief**:
The block of personal facts at the top of the cv: name, contacts, location, role, languages.
_Avoid_: header, bio, about

**Tool**:
One technology dima uses, shown as an icon chip.
_Avoid_: skill, tech, badge

**Tool group**:
A named set of tools, such as core, state or LLM.
_Avoid_: category, section

**Portfolio**:
The list of jobs, each with dates, employer, role and a short story.
_Avoid_: experience, projects, work history

**Cover**:
The short letter on `/cover`: who dima is, what he looks for, how to reach him.
_Avoid_: bio, intro, about page
