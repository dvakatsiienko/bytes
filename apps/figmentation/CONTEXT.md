# figmentation

A showcase of design studies: each one a real site redrawn first in figma, then in code, to practise both.

## Language

**Study**:
One recreated page with its own route, its own palette and its own figma file; studies share nothing.
_Avoid_: demo, project, experiment

**Study card**:
The tile on the home page that stands for one study and links to it and to its figma file.
_Avoid_: project card, preview

**Figma file**:
The design a study was drawn from before it was coded; the code follows it, not the other way round.
_Avoid_: mockup, prototype

**Work in progress**:
A study still being built; its card shows only in development, while its route stays reachable.
_Avoid_: draft, hidden study
