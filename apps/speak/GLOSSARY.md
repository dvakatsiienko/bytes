# speak — glossary

- **engine** — a way to turn text into speech: elevenlabs, kokoro, fish, gemini, or the macOS voice
- **catalogue** — the daemon's list of its engines: each one's label, languages and voices
- **chain** — one language's engines in the order the daemon tries them; a failing engine hands the line to the next
- **voice** — the speaker an engine uses, chosen per language
- **favourite** — a voice marked ♥ for an engine; favourites sort first in that engine's list; the daemon writes them on a heart, and no read asks them
- **speed** — playback rate, pitch kept; 1 is the engine's own pace
- **gain** — loudness multiplier on an engine's audio; 1 is as sent
- **sample line** — the text ▶ speaks for a column; it is never saved
- **preview** — the daemon speaking a sample line with a card's unsaved settings
- **first audio budget** — how long a cloud engine may take to start speaking before the chain moves on
- **benched** — an engine the daemon skips for a while after a quota, auth or network failure, or a hang of kokoro's server
- **no quota** — an engine its provider reports out of credits, found by the daemon's quota probe (at start, on a status older than 10 min, right after a refused request); benched until the provider's refill date (elevenlabs) or for an hour (fish's api balance), and cleared once that time passed, when the probe sees more than at the refusal, or when the engine next succeeds
- **x-speak** — the resident daemon (`daemon/`) that owns the hotkeys (F4 reads, or pauses / resumes with nothing selected; ⇧F4 pauses / resumes; F5 stops), the engines and the settings file
- **one output** — the daemon's audio engine, which every voice plays through
- **playback** — what the daemon plays: nothing, or one numbered read, running or paused; the pill, the player and `status` all read it
- **read** — one text spoken from its first chunk to its last; each has a number, and `status` names the one that plays
- **pill** — the small floating window x-speak shows while it speaks: its wave, ⏸ / ▶, ■, stick and close; it keeps its spot per display
- **pill glide** — how long the pill's bars take to reach a new level, in ms (`meterGlideMs`)
- **pill flow** — how long the pill's wave holds before moving one bar outward, in ms (`meterFlowMs`)
- **word lists** — the words, letters, units, file kinds and ⌘C-only apps a read treats their own way: what ships in `core/word-lists.swift`, and dima's `word-lists.json` beside the settings file over it
- **settings file** — dima's knobs on this mac, `~/Library/Application Support/speak/config.json`; the daemon alone writes it
- **settings version** — the mark of the settings file's bytes; a save names the one it edits, and is refused when the file moved on
- **kept binary** — the daemon a build replaced, held beside the installed one as `x-speak.prev` with the config argument and the settings it ran with
- **rollback** — the kept binary and the installed one change places and the daemon restarts; a second one comes forward again
- **start mark** — the line a daemon writes once its settings are loaded and its socket and main loop run: `ok`, `no-settings` or `no-f4` (macos refused it F4), its build's hash and the time
- **read record** — the line a finished read leaves: when, from which app, words and seconds heard, the engines, how it ended; no word of the read
- **heard / position** — how long a read was listened to, and how far into its sound it is; at 1.5× the second runs faster
- **master volume** — one share, 0 to 100 percent, over every engine's gain
- **kept sound** — a sentence's rendered sound, held in the daemon's memory to be heard again with no render
- **hot / cold read** — a cold one is the first 5 minutes or more after the last one's end, or after the daemon's start; the rest are hot
- **pill state** — one of the ten fixed things a pill can show, behind its sealed boards; a try run shows one or prints it
- **try run** — one text spoken by a tree's own daemon build, beside the live daemon
